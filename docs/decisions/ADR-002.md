# ADR-002: Lasso records

Status: Proposed (approve or change before Task 25)
Date: 2026-11-12

## Context
Lasso keeps 265 `lasso-urls` posts (263 published, 1 trashed, 1 `lasso_delete`), with their data spread over
post meta, `wp_lasso_url_details`, `wp_lasso_amazon_products` and `wp_lasso_field_mapping`. 9 published
records have a title and nothing else, and 7 Amazon records have no cached Amazon row (Task 4,
`tools/README.md`). Lasso stays active on production until cutover (Task 41). The alternative weighed is to
adopt the `lasso-urls` posts in place.

## Decision
Copy each published record into a new `affibloom_product`, keeping the Lasso post ID in
`_affibloom_lasso_id`. Lasso's posts and tables are only read, never changed.

## Why
Rollback stays a matter of switching plugins: Lasso's data is untouched. Adopting the posts in place would
tie Affibloom's fields to Lasso's meta names and leave both plugins writing the same rows while both are active.

## Consequences
The migration needs an ID map (`LassoIdMap`) so `[lasso id="…"]`, clicks and usage can find the new product.
The 9 records with no link are skipped and listed in the migration report. Records added in Lasso after the
first run are picked up by re-running the migration (`--update`) at cutover.
