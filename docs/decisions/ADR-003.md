# ADR-003: Existing boxes

Status: Proposed (approve or change before Task 27)
Date: 2026-11-12

## Context
250 boxes in 34 published posts: 248 `affiliate-plugin/lasso` blocks wrapping a `[lasso …]` shortcode and
2 bare `[lasso …]` shortcodes (Task 4). They use 11 shortcode attributes, including per-box title, image and
URL overrides. The alternatives weighed are: keep rendering the old shortcode only, convert to the new block
only, or both.

## Decision
Both. While Lasso is inactive, a compatibility handler renders `[lasso]` (and the legacy block) from the
migrated products. At cutover the legacy blocks are converted to `affibloom/product-box`, and each post's
original content is kept in `_affibloom_pre_conversion` so the conversion can be reverted.

## Why
The compatibility handler means no guide breaks the moment Lasso is switched off, and it covers anything the
conversion misses. Conversion gives editors a block they can edit, with no dependency on Lasso's markup.

## Consequences
Every attribute in use must be mapped (Task 27's table, including `title_url` and `title_type` from the
inventory). The conversion runs once per post and is reversible with `--revert`.
