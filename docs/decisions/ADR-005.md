# ADR-005: Click storage

Status: Proposed (approve or change before Task 33)
Date: 2026-11-12

## Context
Lasso counts clicks per product (2,839 in total across all records on the Local copy) and shows them on the
Dashboard. The alternative weighed is one row per click.

## Decision
Daily totals per product, post and button in `{prefix}affibloom_clicks`, kept 400 days. No IP address, user
agent or other personal data is stored. A 30-second repeat guard (`affibloom_c_<hash>` transient) stops a
double click from counting twice.

## Why
The Dashboard and Locations tab need totals, not individual clicks. Daily rows keep the table small and leave
nothing personal to protect or delete.

## Consequences
Questions like "which hour" or "which visitor" cannot be answered later. The repeat guard keys on a hash that
is not stored beyond 30 seconds.
